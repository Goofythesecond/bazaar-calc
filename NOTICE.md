## Community research
The bazaar daily-limit behaviour (15B/day, reset 00:00 UTC, which actions count, per-action integer cap) follows the
open-source [SkyHanni](https://github.com/hannibal002/SkyHanni) and [Bazaar Utils](https://github.com/mkram17/Bazaar-Utils)
mods. No code was copied; only the documented behaviour was used.
